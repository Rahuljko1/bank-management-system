About Smart Bank

A learning project that demonstrates a digital-banking interface.

What this prototype covers

Account views, transaction history, profile edits and a fictional transfer flow backed by local data.

What it deliberately avoids

No real balances, billers, payment cards, investments, contact channel or customer-support queue are connected.

How to use it

Use the screens to study common banking-interface patterns without entering real financial information.

Project note

This Smart Bank website is an educational frontend project. Account data, balances, and transfers shown here are examples only.
